What Is Universal Life Insurance?

Universal Life Insurance is a flexible and permanent life insurance policy that offers both a death benefit and the potential for cash accumulation. It combines the protection of traditional life insurance with a savings component that grows over time.

With Universal Life Insurance, you have the flexibility to adjust your premium payments and death benefit coverage as your financial situation changes over time — making it ideal for those whose income or needs may shift throughout their lives.

Key Benefits

Flexible Premiums

Adjust your premium payments to match your budget and financial goals — increase, decrease, or even skip payments within policy limits.

Cash Value Growth

A portion of your premiums builds a cash value account that earns interest over time — accessible through policy loans or withdrawals.

Death Benefit Protection

Provides a death benefit to protect your loved ones financially, helping replace lost income, cover debts, and maintain their lifestyle.

Tax Advantages

Cash value growth is tax-deferred. The death benefit is generally paid income-tax-free to your beneficiaries.

Who Can Benefit?

Universal Life Insurance can be beneficial for a wide range of individuals and families:

  • Families needing long-term financial protection with flexible budgeting
  • Individuals with estate planning needs who want to preserve and transfer wealth
  • Those seeking flexibility and customization in their life insurance coverage
  • Business owners planning for succession or key-person insurance
